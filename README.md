
Dear Data 100 - Project Planning

 1. Project Title
The art of my spending 

2. Research Question
How have my spending habits changed throughout the year, and what do I spend the most money on?

I want to find out:

How my spending changes from month to month.

what i spend the most on, such as food, transport, shopping and entertainment.

Whether there are particular months when I spend significantly more or less.

What patterns I can discover about my spending habits.

 3. Data Sources
I collected my spending data from Nationwide, American Express and Capital One statements, as these are the three main accounts I use for purchases.

I chose these as they record of my transactions over time, allowing me to investigate my spending habits.

4. Data Collection


id: Unique number for each observation
month: Month of the transaction
amount_band: Approximate spending range
category: Type of spending
source: Nationwide, Amex or Capital One

5. Visualisation Ideas


I plan to create a **Spending Galaxy**, where each star represents a purchase. The colours will represent spending categories, the sizes will show spending ranges, and the spiral will show how my spending changes over time.
